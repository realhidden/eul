Scenario

Try: drag the pressure slider and watch the width governor respond — NET drops first (priority order), then CPU, then the strip is gone and the anchor holds the floor; past that, macOS hides eul entirely and the recovery notification fires (doc §2.5). Click the anchor (the eyes) to open the panel; click the CPU tile to expand per-core detail; click the FANS tile to expand it, then Control fans… walks the helper-install ceremony (doc §4.6). Set a fan to Manual or Boost and the FAN slot pins itself into the bar — the override you can't forget. Click any unit in the panel (MB/s, °C) to flip it everywhere — personalization at the point of use (doc §4.7). Esc or click-away dismisses the panel.

The slider and these buttons are doc chrome, not product UI. Design tokens are named constants in prototype.js (T) and prototype.css (--tk-*); web-freedom assumptions are flagged with WEB-FREEDOM comments in both files.
