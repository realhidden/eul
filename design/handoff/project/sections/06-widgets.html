Widget strategy

Two widgets instead of four, both honest about time. WidgetKit budgets reloads, and eul coalesces them to protect the energy budget — so freshness is 15–60 s, and the design says so instead of pretending.

Theming follows the system (WidgetKit constraint — accepted, not fought). Configurability (choose monitors per widget, via App Intents) is designed-for but deferred to v2.x new infra 14+. Timeline entries carry their capture timestamp so the relative stamp renders without waking the app new infra.
