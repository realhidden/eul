Phasing

v2.0 is the revamp: the bar model, the panel, and the health engine. v2.x is depth on the same skeleton — no surface added later, only filled in.

ReleaseShipsSized for
v2.0Anchor + strip, width governor, health engine, panel (with per-core CPU, sparklines, processes), recovery flow, settings rebuild, token palette dark/light, App Store degradation, i18n hardening. Should-haves if green: fan control (manual + Boost, helper ceremony, removal flow), the two widgets, BT battery tile, process icons.Small community team, months. The riskiest items (occlusion detection, two-item ordering) are prototyped first — they gate the architecture (§10, asks 3–4).
v2.xFan curves / smart cooling (only after manual control earns trust), widget configurability, sensors surface, CSV export, network identity monitors (IP/SSID/Rosetta), disk I/O tile, panel pop-out, Audio Graphs.One theme per point release; each is additive to an existing surface, none changes the IA.

macOS floor — recommendation, not fact

Design against 14; ratify at review. Elements that hard-require 13+/14+ are tagged 14+ throughout. The honest retreat cost to 13: none of the IA changes; to 12 or lower: the panel host (MenuBarExtra window style), modern widget configurability, and Audio Graph support all need bespoke replacements — at that point the floor is cheaper to raise than the workarounds are to maintain. The App Store/no-SMC question is orthogonal to the floor and handled entirely in §3's must row.
